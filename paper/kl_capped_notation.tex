\caption{Reward function notation}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Symbol & Description \\
\midrule
$r_{i}(t)$ & Reward for selecting client $i$ at round $t$ \\
$\Delta a_{i}$ & Per-client accuracy change: $a_{i}(t) - a_{i}(t-1)$ \\
$a_{\text{prev}}$ & Global mean accuracy before round $t$ \\
$\varepsilon$ & Small constant to avoid division by zero \\
$\bar{a}$ & Mean accuracy across all clients \\
$a_{i}$ & Client $i$'s current accuracy \\
$\gamma$ & Fairness pressure coefficient \\
$D_{\text{KL}}(P_{i} \| P_{g})$ & KL divergence of client $i$'s class distribution from global \\
$\alpha$ & KL divergence weight \\
$\beta$ & Participation penalty weight \\
$f_{i}$ & Participation frequency of client $i$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\label{tab:notation}
